      05 / THE HUMAN LAYER

      DATA ONLY
      MATTERS WHEN
      IT REACHES PEOPLE.

      A warning can be technically correct and still fail if it is late, unclear, inaccessible or not trusted. The last mile is part of the technology story.

      01 / DATA

      Observe

      Satellites, sensors, weather stations and geographic datasets create the evidence base.

      02 / DECISION

      Interpret

      Forecasts and maps are turned into decisions by agencies, responders and local institutions.

      03 / COMMUNITY

      Act

      Warnings matter only when people receive, understand and act on them.

      END-TO-END RESILIENCE

      The final interface is not a screen. It is the connection between information, institutions and people.

      06 / LIMITS & FUTURE

      TECHNOLOGY
      IS NOT MAGIC.

      More data does not automatically mean less risk. The next generation of systems has to be more connected, transparent, accessible and useful in real conditions.

      Future direction: stronger interoperability, better local data, explainable analytics, resilient communications and systems designed around the people who must use them under pressure.
      07 / THE TAKEAWAY

      RESILIENCE
      IS A CHAIN.

      Break one link and the whole system becomes weaker. Strengthen the links together and technology becomes a practical part of disaster risk reduction.
